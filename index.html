02 / Research

Research

Deep learning for satellite imagery, Pandit Deendayal Energy University.

IEEE ICETA 2025 · published Aug 2025

Harnessing Generative Adversarial Models to Enhance Satellite Imagery Quality

Implemented SRGAN and ESRGAN to upscale low-resolution satellite images, and measured the trade-off between pixel-level accuracy (PSNR) and perceptual quality (LPIPS). Applied to urban planning, environmental monitoring and solar-panel defect detection.

Read the paper (DOI) ↗
Research intern · Jan–May 2025

Image Segmentation for Map Generation and Deforestation Detection

Built U-Net, FCN-8 and a Dual-Stream U-Net for satellite segmentation and multi-temporal change detection. U-Net beat FCN-8 on PSNR and SSIM for map generation; the dual-stream model reached 89% IoU detecting deforestation.
